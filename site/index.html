How it works

1

Seed

Pre-populate mock state with your test data. CRM leads, Drive files, calendar events.

2

Run

Point your agent at the mock server. Every tool call hits real handlers backed by SQLite.

3

Assert

Check the resulting state. Was the email sent? Was the sheet created? Was the right tool called?

4

Compare

Run baseline before your change, revision after. The report shows what improved and what regressed.
