What the hill climb did

Round 1 generated a "language-match-and-style" variant that added explicit language detection rules to the system prompt. This fixed 10.8% of language_match failures (bot responding in wrong language in multilingual group chats) and improved task_completion by 10.9% (bot now actually executes tool calls instead of describing what it would do).
